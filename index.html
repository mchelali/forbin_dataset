The Forbin Dataset

A collection of historical photographs enriched with archival metadata and semantic annotations

Explore the Forbin Dataset — a curated collection of heritage photographs and documents, combining digitized facsimiles, historical context, and fine-grained annotations.

This collection brings together images from the archives of the French explorer and writer Victor Forbin (1868–1947), enhanced with semantic structures following the COCO Segmentation format for scientific reuse.
